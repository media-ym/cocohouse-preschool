Why parents enquire

One preschool & daycare for learning and a full working day

Cocohouse is a thematic preschool in Hari Om Nagar. Children learn through stories, Coding Phonics, CPA Maths and the Panchakosha approach, aligned with NEP and NCF 2022.

If you need care beyond school hours, daycare stays open from 8 AM to 8 PM. Most centres close earlier. Cocohouse kept this 12-hour window so a parent can finish the office day and still reach a child who has eaten, rested and, for older children, finished homework.

The preschool & daycare uses CCTV. Pickup is verified, and working parents can get a WhatsApp update during the day.

Questions parents ask

Before you book the visit

What does the 60% off cover?

It is the current mid-term admission offer for 2026-27. The amount depends on the program. The team confirms the fee on the phone before you visit, so you are not guessing from the website.

Which ages can join now?

Playgroup starts at 2 years, Nursery at 3, Junior KG at 4 and Senior KG at 5. Daycare takes children from 1.5 years to 10 years. Inclusive Special Education admissions are open up to 10 years, with a separate time and teacher.

Can daycare cover a full office day?

Yes. Daycare is open 8 AM to 8 PM, seven days, for working parents. You can use it with preschool on the same preschool & daycare, or only for after-school hours.

Where is the preschool & daycare?

Row House No 3, Millenium Park, Hari Om Nagar, Thane East, Mumbai-400081. Families from Thane East and Mulund visit this preschool & daycare. Call +91-7039152173 if you want directions before you leave home.

How soon will someone call?

Enquiry calls are returned within 24 hours. For a faster answer, call or WhatsApp 7039152173. The office answers Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 9 PM.
