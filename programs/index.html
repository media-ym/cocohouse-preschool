Our Programs

From first steps to school readiness — discover the carefully designed programs that nurture children aged 1.5 to 8 years at every stage of their early learning journey.

Six Programs · One Philosophy

Detailed Program Breakdown

🌼

Playgroup

Age: 2 – 3 Years

Duration: 2.5 hours/day

Enquire Now →

The very first step into the world of structured learning — designed to make the transition from home to school gentle, joyful and full of wonder. Our Playgroup program focuses on sensory exploration, motor skill development and social-emotional growth through play-based activities that feel more like fun than learning.

Learning Outcomes

  • Sensorial & fine motor skill development through Montessori-inspired materials
  • Story-based learning that builds listening, attention and vocabulary
  • Social interaction & confidence building through group play and circle time
  • Independence in basic self-help skills (eating, washing, tidying up)
  • Exposure to rhymes, music, dance and creative art every single day

11:15 AM Story Time & Goodbye

Nursery

Age: 3 – 4 Years

Duration: 3 hours/day

Enquire Now →

Where curiosity meets structure. The Nursery program builds on the foundations of Playgroup and introduces early literacy and numeracy through our signature Coding Phonics method and theme-based exploration. Children learn to recognise letters, numbers, colours and shapes — all while having an absolute blast.

Learning Outcomes

  • Coding Phonics introduction — 26 letter sounds with actions and stories
  • Pre-writing & language skills through tracing, sandpaper letters and air writing
  • Theme-based exploration of seasons, animals, community helpers and festivals
  • Number recognition (1-20), counting, patterns and basic shapes
  • Gross motor development through structured outdoor play and dance

10:45 AM Maths & Numbers
11:15 AM Art / Music / Outdoor
11:45 AM Story Time & Goodbye

Junior KG

Age: 4 – 5 Years

Duration: 3.5 hours/day

Enquire Now →

The leap into formal learning begins here. Junior KG introduces children to the complete 44-sound Coding Phonics system and the Concrete-Pictorial-Abstract (CPA) Maths methodology — a Singapore-inspired approach that builds genuine mathematical understanding rather than rote memorisation.

Learning Outcomes

  • Advanced Coding Phonics — all 44 sounds with blending & segmenting
  • CPA Maths methodology — Concrete to Pictorial to Abstract progression
  • Reading simple CVC words and basic sight vocabulary
  • Numbers 1-100, addition to 10, subtraction to 5, patterns and time
  • Creative thinking through project-based thematic units

Senior KG

Age: 5 – 6 Years

Duration: 4 hours/day

Enquire Now →

The final stretch before formal schooling. Senior KG consolidates all prior learning and prepares children for the rigours of Grade 1 — building reading fluency, writing confidence, problem-solving skills and the social-emotional maturity needed for big school.

Learning Outcomes

  • Reading fluency with simple sentences and short story books
  • Writing skills — capital & lowercase letters, numbers 1-1000, simple words
  • Problem solving & logical thinking through puzzles and games
  • Addition & subtraction to 20, multiplication tables (2, 5, 10), money & time
  • Comprehensive School Readiness Program — interview prep, classroom etiquette

Inclusive Special Education

Age: 1 – 8 Years

Duration: Individual Assessment

Enquire Now →

Every child deserves to bloom — at their own pace, in their own way. Our Inclusive Special Education program provides personalised support for children with ASD, speech & communication delays, learning difficulties, behavioural challenges and developmental delays.

Learning Outcomes

  • Personalised learning plans tailored to each child's unique needs
  • Trained & certified special educators with 5+ years of experience
  • Speech therapy, occupational therapy & behavioural support (on request)
  • Holistic development approach — academic, social, emotional & life skills
  • Regular parent-teacher meetings and home-program recommendations
