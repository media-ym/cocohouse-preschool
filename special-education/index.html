A look at the session

One teacher. One child. Own time.

Special education at Cocohouse is a separate session. These photos show that one-to-one way of learning: picture cards, hands-on play, and a teacher sitting with the child. The children are not in the regular preschool batch.

Who this is for

Children we support

Every child is different. The family speaks with the special educator first. The seat, the time and the plan are confirmed after that conversation. Admissions are open up to 10 years.

01Autism

Children on the autism spectrum who need a calmer, more predictable session. Learning is one step at a time, with their own teacher, not inside the busy preschool class.

02ADHD

Children who find a long group class hard to sit through. The session is shorter and clearer, in a separate time, with one teacher guiding the activity.

03Speech and communication

Children who are still building words, gestures, or picture-based communication. The teacher uses pictures, play and a pace that matches the child.

04Learning difficulty

Children who need a slower pace than nursery or KG. The lesson is planned for that child. It is not the regular preschool timetable.

05Behaviour

Children who find a big group overwhelming. The session stays small and separate, so the teacher can keep the day steady.

06Down syndrome

Children with Down syndrome learn in their own session, with their own teacher. Play, pictures and daily skills follow that child's pace, not the regular preschool class.

07Developmental delay

Children who need more time for play, daily skills and learning. Cocohouse takes these admissions up to 10 years, after speaking with the family.

Why Cocohouse is different

Inclusive Special Education, kept separate from the preschool batch

Cocohouse runs this program inside the same preschool and daycare, but the children are not put into the regular playgroup, nursery or KG class.

01

02

A separate teacher

03

A separate way of learning

Cocohouse takes Inclusive Special Education admissions up to 10 years of age. The team first talks to the family, understands the child, and then confirms whether a seat and a plan are right.

Questions families ask

Are these children in the same class as other preschool children?

No. Inclusive Special Education at Cocohouse has a separate time. The children are not mixed into playgroup, nursery, Junior KG or Senior KG for that session.

Who teaches them?

A separate special educator. The regular preschool class teacher does not take this session.

Is the learning the same as the preschool class?

No. The learning is planned separately for the child. Activities and goals follow that plan, not the regular preschool timetable.

Which children do you support?

Autism, ADHD, Down syndrome, speech and communication delay, learning difficulty, behaviour needs, and developmental delay. The special educator confirms the plan after speaking with the family. These children are not mixed into the regular preschool class.

Till what age do you take admissions?

Up to 10 years. The team confirms the seat after speaking with the family.

Where does this happen?

At the same Cocohouse address: Row House No 3, Millenium Park, Hari Om Nagar, Mulund East, Mumbai-400081.
