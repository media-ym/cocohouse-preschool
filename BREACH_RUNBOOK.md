# Personal-data breach runbook

Draft for lawyer review. This is an internal checklist and two letter templates. It is not legal advice, and it does not mean a notice has been given.

Use this when personal data held by the school (enquiry forms, consent records, data-rights requests, or staff accounts) is lost, stolen, shared with the wrong person, or otherwise breached.

## First hour

1. One person leads. Write the time you first knew about the breach.
2. Stop further spread. Change the admin password. Do not delete the evidence.
3. Note what was involved: parent name, phone, email, child name, child age, special-education condition, or staff login. Note how many people, and whether it left the school (email, hosting panel, a lost laptop, a public link).
4. Do not put the affected people’s data into a new email thread while describing the incident.

## Within 72 hours of becoming aware

The school should tell the Data Protection Board of India, and tell each affected person, as the DPDP Act and the current rules require. A lawyer should confirm the clock, the form, and where the Board notice is filed before the first real incident. The templates below are drafts to fill in. They are not a filing.

Keep a copy of what was sent, when, and to whom.

4. Nature of the personal data: [for example parent name and mobile; child name and age; special-education condition named on a form]

6. Likely consequences: [for example unwanted calls, or disclosure of a child’s support need]

## Draft notice to an affected parent

Subject: A notice about your information at Cocohouse

Dear [parent name],

We are writing because personal data you gave Cocohouse Preschool & Daycare may have been [what happened: for example sent to the wrong person / accessed without permission] on [date].

The information involved is: [list only the categories for this person, such as your name, mobile number, your child’s name and age]. We are not repeating those details in this message.

What we have done: [for example closed the access, reset the staff password, asked the recipient to delete the message].

What you can do: watch for unexpected calls or messages that use your child’s name. You can ask us to access, correct, or erase what we hold, or to withdraw consent, at https://cocohouse.in/data-rights or by email to info@cocohouse.in or phone +91-7039152173.

We became aware of this on [date and time]. This note is sent so you can protect yourself. It is not an admission beyond the facts above.
